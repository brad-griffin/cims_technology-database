Fix classes: **A** = a safe data fix (records only), **B** = needs code, **C** = needs a modeller ruling. Where a row lists two classes, the first one does most of the fix.

Two findings point to code defects that reach beyond their own archetypes:
- **Double heating-value factor on per-tonne hydrogen emissions (HPCH017, RX011).** 32 unexcluded emissions rows convert t CO2 per t H2 with both `cf-h2-hhv-mass` and `cf-hhv-lhv-hydrogen`, so they come out 18% low. For example, Collodi's 9 t/t becomes 0.0537 t/GJ where it should be 0.0635. Archetypes affected: steam-reformer_methane (12 rows), separator_biogas-upgrading (6), gasifier_coal-petroleum-coke and its ccs variant (3 each), steam-reformer_methane_ccs-concentrated (3), autothermal-reformer_hydrogen (2), and one row each on hydrotreating-lipids, methane-pyrolysis and the low-temperature electrolyzer. A mass basis needs only the mass HHV factor, so the regional LHV default should not apply to these rows. Rows printed per Nm3 or per lb get only one factor, which is why the SMR parts and total disagree.
- **One conversion factor per example (IS006).** `convert.Factors.for_row` attaches a factor to the whole example. An example that has both an H2 m3/t row and a natural-gas m3/t row therefore cannot convert both.
