# Sanity-check pass (2026-10-04)

Review of every pooled value in `database/harmonized/output/archetype-parameter.csv` (3,752 rows; 3,150 sourced, 540 estimated, 62 identity) across the 535 archetypes, against the build of 2026-10-04 06:12 (working tree on top of 2690970, including the other sessions' uncommitted intakes). Three passes were run:

1. a statistical screen of the sourced rows — high/low ratio, where the central sits in its range, and per-parameter plausibility gates (efficiency, capacity factor, lifetime, construction time, zero or negative costs);
2. a family-by-family comparison of every archetype's values with the literature (IEA, ETSAP, DEA, NREL ATB, BREFs, LBNL/DOE bandwidth studies, engineering relations), run as seven parallel reviews over the per-family summaries;
3. a disposition of the set-aside items S01–S70 in `database/reviews/sanity-checks/sanity-check-list.csv`.

Findings are in `database/reviews/sanity-checks/2026-10-04/review.csv` (673 rows: 92 high, 258 medium, 323 low). The first 21 rows (R001–R021, `verified = yes`) were traced to the example rows and the pipeline; the rest (`verified = no`) come from the family reviews and were spot-checked, not individually traced. Nothing in the repository was changed by this pass except `database/reviews/sanity-checks/sanity-check-list.csv` (statuses and new rows S71–S88) and the two files named here.

The short version: a handful of pipeline and loader defects, each small, produce most of the values that look absurd; the remaining problems are scope (whole-plant or whole-route numbers pooled into unit-process archetypes, savings pooled as absolutes, stack lives pooled as plant lives) rather than wrong digits. The centrals of the big, well-sourced archetypes (boilers, kilns, EAF, chlor-alkali, SMR, amine capture, electrolysis, lime, aluminium) are in range; the ranges around them are what carry the errors.

## 2. Scope defects (systematic; need a rule or a per-example ruling)

These are not wrong digits; they are values that belong to a different thing than the archetype they sit on. Each was confirmed on at least one example in the pass; the counts are from the family reviews.

- **Whole-plant or whole-route values on unit-process archetypes.** Steel: UKERC, Link 2008, Otto 2017 and IISI 1998 per-t-crude-steel route values on the blast-furnace, TGR-BF, DRI and CCS variants (TGR-BF total 20.5 GJ/t; DRI-CCS 15.8). Pulp: IEAGHG 2016, Francis 2002, Ekbom 2003 per-ADt values on the per-t-black-liquor-solids boilers and gasifier (black liquor 21–35 GJ/t BLS, above the ~14 GJ/t HHV of the solids) and on the lime kiln (1.2 GJ/t CaO, below the 3.18 GJ/t calcination enthalpy); whole-mill capex on the digester (3,869 CAD/t-yr), press section, wet end, debarker and lumber kiln. Non-ferrous: cusano 2017 smelter capex on electrorefining, converting and flash smelting; Gielen 1997 per-t-Al chain energy on the Bayer digester (20.4 GJ/t) and the prebaked cell (23 GJ/t fuel). Refining: MIDDEN whole-refinery electrification on direct-heater_electrified-crude (3.12 GJ/t); whole-refinery and per-t-H2 outliers in hydroprocessing (30.7 GJ/t; 1.61 t/t). Food: Rehfeldt 2018 per-t-sugar on the per-t-beet refinery. Chemicals: Agora 2021 per-t-steel values on reactor_steel-offgas-chemicals (per t CO2); Boulamanti 2017 plant-level values on the distillation columns. The fix is per example (a `ratio` conversion factor where the source gives the ratio, exclusion otherwise); the per-ADt -> per-t-BLS case could be a named factor since three sources need it.
- **Capture energy and cost on the host-with-capture archetypes.** The `_ccs-diffuse` and `_ccs-concentrated` host archetypes (cement, lime, BF, DRI, recovery boiler, SMR) carry the reboiler steam and compression electricity (cement 3.35 GJ/t steam; BF 1.14; recovery boiler 5.9) that the descriptions assign to the capture-service archetypes. Costs are already set aside (S68); energy should follow the same rule, or the description should be changed to whole-route.
- **MIDDEN residual emissions on CCS variants near zero** (TGR-BF 0.049, DRI-CCS 0.028, electrowinning 0.012 t/t) and, being top-ranked, they set the central; share-to-capture (90%) contradicts the captured tonnes (35–50%). Treat MIDDEN `emissions-total` on CCS archetypes as unusable until its scope is read off the cards.
- **Carrier sub-shares pooled as independent populations.** `[natural-gas]`, `[fuel]`, `[by-product-gas]`, `[coke-oven-gas]` and the untagged total are each the median of a different example set, so the carriers sum to 1.5–3x the total in the coke oven, BF, cement kilns and glass furnaces, and the total sometimes falls below one carrier (steam-reformer_methane total 0.651 < natural-gas 1.23). This is the design (one median per target) rather than an error, but a model that sums carriers over-counts fuel. Either publish a consistency flag per archetype (sum of carriers vs total) or derive the total from the carriers of the top-ranked example only.
- **Efficiency-modifier archetypes above their base.** Because the `_efficiency-moderate/strong` buckets pool absolute post-measure levels from sources with other baselines (defect 5), the ladders invert in coke oven, BF, paper dryer, bakery oven, spray dryer, ceramic kiln, cracker and reformer. They also carry whole-plant capex (kiln_dry-precalciner_efficiency-moderate 857 CAD/t-yr = 1.7x a new kiln line; cracker efficiency-moderate 1,207; ammonia efficiency-strong 839 = baseline). Deriving the modifiers from the base archetype (saving fraction; incremental cost) fixes both.
- **LHV/HHV not applied on some European sources.** Boiler efficiencies to 110%, CHP totals to 106%, CCGT to 60% are LHV figures on an HHV database; the `cf-hhv-lhv-*` factors exist but are not applied where the source does not state a basis. A per-source default basis (DEA, ETSAP, BREF, MIDDEN = LHV unless stated) would remove a +5–10 point bias on efficiency and the matching bias on SEC.
- **Heat pumps pool closed-cycle and open-cycle (MVR).** heat-pump_industrial efficiency 76–3,000% (n=129), central 350; distillation-column_heat-pumped 110–1,145%. The central is a closed-cycle COP, the range is not. A species (closed / vapour-recompression) would let the model see both.
- **Stack, membrane, campaign and measure-horizon lives in plant lifetime** (defect 4 plus MIDDEN 1-, 5- and 10-year horizons; glass campaign lives 2–7 yr; aluminium pot lives 4–6 yr; LDAR 1.5 yr). The sibling-copy fill rule then spreads them.
- **Variable-output capex on two bases.** Wind 29 vs 178 CAD/GJ-yr is the same turbine per rated vs per delivered output; PV+battery, solar collector and CSP are affected; thermal-storage_electric capex per GJ of storage vs per GJ delivered. The `capacity-factor` scaling in normalise applies only where the source gives one.
- **Emission factors on an input basis in output fields.** EIA 2024 fuel factors per GJ fuel (0.050 t/GJ) sit on combined-cycle and simple-cycle beside per-GJ-electricity values; CHP factors per GJ electricity without heat credit; steam-turbine_condensing 0.55 t/GJ is a steelworks plant burning blast-furnace gas (Pardo & Moya 2013), not a general factor. Deriving combustion CO2 from SEC x carrier factor (the identity rule already exists) and using the sourced value only as a check would be more consistent.

## 4. Family highlights from the comparison pass

Values that anchor the database and check out: boiler and CHP capex and efficiencies at the centrals; kiln_dry-precalciner fuel 3.43 GJ/t, process 0.525 and total 0.83 t/t clinker; lime kilns (PFR 3.75, preheater 5.4, long rotary 6.6 GJ/t; process CO2 0.785 and 0.913 t/t exact); prebaked 48 and Soderberg 61 GJ/t Al; chlor-alkali electricity by cell type; SMR ammonia 32.6 GJ/t and 1.93 t CO2/t; e-ammonia 35.5 GJ/t; CO2-to-methanol hydrogen 27.2 GJ/t; amine capture 3.5–4.5 GJ/t and 350–470 CAD per t/yr; N2O factors for adipic and nitric acid; refinery unit SEC centrals; methane-measure capex at the Natural Gas STAR partner level.

Per family, the issues that matter most for a model (full rows in the CSV):

- **Heat supply, power, drives.** The Btu/kWh and cent/mill defects; UK measure sources filed as SEC and emission factors; motors carry whole-system efficiencies (pump 40%, fan 40%, compressed air 6.4%) so motor SEC reads 2.5 GJ/GJ; wind and solar capex on two bases; thermal-storage_electric capex 3.5 CAD/GJ-yr looks 10–25x low; DEA 2026 construction times of 0.001–0.04 yr and solar O&M 0.0039 CAD/GJ-yr look like unit slips.
- **Capture, hydrogen, biofuels.** Johansson 2012 per-t-product emissions on capture-unit_amine (captured 1.28 t/t, combustion 1.5, co2e -0.76); the capture chain's abatement and levelized costs on compressor_co2-dehydration; biofuel product unit "t L or t" unresolved with per-litre values leaked into per-tonne fields (molecular-sieve electricity 2.4e-5 GJ/t); methane-pyrolysis opex-fixed central 0.042 CAD/GJ-yr (x100 low); electrolyzer capex central (~1,000 USD/kW) is a 2030 vintage pooled with current pilots.
- **Iron and steel.** Coke-oven SEC family (feedstock energy, three gas labels for one underfiring duty); route values on unit archetypes; Agora steel-offgas per t steel; MIDDEN CCS residuals; Energetics 2000 whole-shop allocations setting BOF gas, ingot electricity and ladle-furnace centrals; induction reheating 1.8 GJ/t at 85% efficiency delivers twice the enthalpy of steel at 1,200 degC.
- **Cement, minerals, non-ferrous.** co2e = total (defect 6); carrier sub-shares; measure archetypes with whole-plant costs; ball mill capex 444 (to 893) vs VRM 45; Gielen 1997 per-t-Al; glass furnace opex-variable 699 CAD/t (a product cost); kiln_lime_ccs-diffuse reduction potential 9% vs 90% capture; furnace_homogenizing 5.8 GJ/t (0.5 is right); campaign and pot lives in plant lifetime.
- **Chemicals.** MIDDEN x1000 cards; per-t bases (t NH3 vs t H2; t Cl2 vs t product; t ethylene vs t HVC); feedstock vs process energy pooled under the untagged SEC (syngas-methanol 11.4 vs 36.5); electrified routes with and without electrolysis in one electricity target; co2e companions not derived from the N2O factors; export-steam sign conventions mixed.
- **Refining, oil and gas.** The two hydrogen archetypes on the wrong unit; [ch4] abatement costs (defect 3); compressor_instrument-air priced per m3 CH4 avoided against a throughput capacity (a 203 M CAD system); pump_plunger-lift capacity 0.37 t CH4/yr per well (30–50x low, inflating capex to 45,709 CAD/t-yr against its own 66 CAD/t CO2e abatement cost); turboexpander fuel 879–1,465 GJ/GJ (x1000); 24 of 73 archetypes have no sourced values.
- **Pulp, food, mining, other.** Per-ADt values on BLS archetypes; whole-mill capex on unit processes; inverted efficiency ladders; flotation 0.13 kWh/t concentrate (100–1,000x low) and ventilation 1.5 kWh/t ore; sugar beet per t sugar; evaporator and dryer product units "t water evaporated or t product" ambiguous; lumber "t or m3".

On S20–S67 (host plants for incremental costs), the pass did not rule; for the record, the obvious pairings are: caster_continuous, electric-arc-furnace, electrolysis-cell_* (chlor-alkali, prebaked, soderberg, inert-anode, oxygen-depolarized), kiln_dry-precalciner, furnace_glass-regenerative, reactor_nitric-acid, reactor_catalytic-reforming, reactor_hydroprocessing, steam-cracker_fired-tubular, smelting-vessel_two-stage, digester_continuous-kraft and reactor_oxygen-delignification are themselves whole plants whose set-aside rows are retrofit measures priced on that same plant (host = the archetype itself); binder_blended-cement and binder_calcined-clay take kiln_dry-precalciner; heat-recovery-unit_* , fan_optimization, pump_optimization, compressor_air-system-management, press_*, pulper_refiner-low-energy, paper-machine_waterless, preheater_ladle and thermal-storage_heat-charged are add-ons whose incremental cost is the right cost (confirm none); gasifier_* cost-levelized rows are per GJ of product and need no host.
