# EPA Natural Gas STAR PRO fact sheets - extraction notes and source defects

Per-document notes from the 2026-10-02 extraction. Every cost and savings figure was checked against the rendered page. No PRO fact sheet prints a price year unless noted; 2011 (revision year) is assumed and flagged.

## PRO 2014 Wet seal degassing recovery system (6 pp, no PRO number)
Extracted: two cases, one compressor and a station with four compressors. Recorded: capex 33,000 and 90,000 (Exh. 3 itemised); opex "Minimal", recorded as 0; capacity of 63 scfm; 99 percent capture.
SOURCE DEFECTS:
- "Annual Methane Savings" of 30,000/120,000 Mcf are whole gas (63 scfm x 8,000 h) valued at the full gas price.
- The Exhibit 4/5 NPVs (280,000 and 1,200,000) are the correct NPV divided by 1.1.
- The partner's 3,300 scfm for 15 compressors implies 220 scfm each, above the stated 185 scfm maximum. 3,300 scfm x 8,500 h = 1.68 Bcf, printed as 1.6 Bcf.
- Costs are labelled $2011 in a 2014 document.
- The text says "Exhibit 3 below presents two scenarios", but the scenarios are in Exhibits 4 and 5.

## PRO 908 Redesign blowdown systems and alter ESD practices (3 pp)
Extracted: two cases.
- YALE closures: capex 8,000 per station with ten 8-inch ESD valves, plus 785-1,600 per device (not installed).
- Valve relocation: negligible at the design stage; 24-inch valve 30,000 + 10,000 installation; 6-inch valve >4,000. Partner savings of 130 and 1,700 Mcf/yr.
SOURCE DEFECTS:
- The basis for the 8,000 table cost is not given.
- The 1,800 Mcf/yr cannot be reproduced from the printed basis (400 Mcf/min relief rate, which is implausible for an 8-inch valve, a 40 scf stack, ten valves, one minute).

## PRO 102 Fewer compressor engine startups and improved ignition (3 pp)
Extracted: two cases.
- Start reduction: no numeric cost ("Minimal training"); 581 Mcf/yr.
- Ignition/controls: capex 1,750 per compressor and 15,000 per station; 800 Mcf/yr.
SOURCE DEFECTS:
- Second table: the values of 6,440 and 4,600 are 920 Mcf of whole gas x price. 920 Mcf of gas is 865 Mcf of methane, not 800. At $3, 920 x 3 = 2,760, but 2,600 is printed (and the 8.1-month payback uses 2,600).
- First table: $7 uses 618 Mcf, while $5 and $3 use 616 Mcf.

## PRO 103 Install electric compressors (2 pp)
Extracted: capex 6,050,000 (five gas compressors totalling 15,561 hp replaced by four 1,750 hp electric units); opex 6,200,000/yr net; capacity 1,750 hp electric-input.
Defects:
- The emission factor is 2.11 in the text but 2.1 in the footnote (minor).
- The opex of 6,200,000 is electricity of 6,800,000 minus about 605,000 of maintenance savings. This is consistent, but the table gives only the net figure.
- An electric compressor efficiency of 50 percent is used as printed.

## PRO 104 Install automated air/fuel ratio controls (3 pp)
Extracted: capex 138,000 (range 98,000-161,000, 2010 USD) per engine; fuel savings of 28,470 Mcf/yr; methane 913-12,175 Mcf/yr.
SOURCE DEFECTS:
- The post-audit reductions read CO2 2,309 TPY and CO 83,300 TPY. These look swapped or mis-stated: 1,450 MMcf/yr of fuel saved implies about 80,000 t CO2.
- Footnote 2 ("2010 dollars") has no marker in the text.
- There is no numeric opex (the doc says extra operating cost is offset by maintenance savings).

## PRO 105 Install electric motor starters (2 pp)
Extracted: capex 1,000-10,000 per starter; opex 100/yr; 1,356 Mcf/yr.
SOURCE DEFECTS:
- The opex of 100/yr is not consistent with the text's 1-5 USD per start x 10 starts (10-50/yr).

## epa_2011_pro-203-pipe-glycol-dehydrator-vapor-recovery-unit (PRO, 2011, 2 pp.)

  - capex $2,000 per dehydrator.

  - Paybacks.
  - The 300/900 scf per MMcf recovery rates and the 7,600 Mcf/yr case without a flash tank.
- **Price year:** none printed; 2011 assumed.

- **Extracted:**
  - Equipment capex $12,500.
  - Installed capex $31,250 (installation = 1.5x equipment).
  - O&M $7,350/yr.
  - 30 hp electric rotary compressor.
  - 180 Mcf/d associated gas at 50% methane, giving 32,850 Mcf/yr methane.
- **Price year:** 2011 assumed.
- **Source defects:**
  - The O&M formula (hp x OF x 8,760 x electricity price) omits the hp-to-kW factor. $7,350 is only reproduced with 0.746 kW/hp.
  - The partner case gives 225 Mcf/d of methane as "total 675 MMcf per year associated gas". 225 x 365 = 82 MMcf.
  - The value of gas assumes 94% methane, but the stream is 50% methane.
  - The printed paybacks (2/3/5 months) are longer than computed (about 1.6/2.2/3.9 months). The $5 row reads "3 Month".

## epa_2011_pro-202-convert-natural-gas-driven-chemical-pumps (3 pp)
- **Extracted:** two cases.
  - Glycol pump converted to instrument air: capex $1,000-10,000 incremental, opex $100-1,000/yr, on a 10 MMcf/d dehydrator, saving 2,500 Mcf/yr.
  - Solar chemical injection pump: $2,000 installed, saving 183 Mcf/yr.
- **SOURCE DEFECTS:**
  - $3/Mcf glycol case: the box value is $7,800. 2,500/0.94 x 3 = about $7,980.
  - Text p.3 gives $12,500/yr at $5 for the glycol pump. That treats methane as whole gas, while the box at 94% gives $13,300.
  - Solar pump: the text (90% methane) gives 203 Mcf, $1,015/yr at $5 and about 2-year payback. The box (94%) gives $900 and 27 months.
  - "9,125 MMcf" total annual methane savings for one partner is implausibly large (9.1 Bcf). It is likely a unit error.

## epa_2011_pro-301-convert-pneumatics-mechanical-controls (3 pp)
- **Extracted:** two cases. Full separator replacement is $3,000 (equipment basis; installation not stated). Replacement net of salvage is $1,000. Opex is $0 and savings are 227 Mcf/yr.
- **SOURCE DEFECTS:**
  - The text basis is 255 Mcf gas at 89% methane ($1,275 at $5). The box uses 94% ($1,200 at $5).
  - At $3, the $3,000 case payback is printed as 47 months. $3,000/$725 x 12 = about 50 months.

## epa_2011_pro-207-portable-desiccant-dehydrators (2 pp)
- **Extracted:** one case. Capex $4,000 (equipment, 10-inch unit). Opex $5,000/yr. Capacity 30 Mcf/d (well flow). Savings 1,891 Mcf/yr methane.
- **SOURCE DEFECTS:**
  - The paybacks (8/11/18 months) equal (capex + one year opex)/value. Netting the recurring $5,000/yr opex against value gives different results (e.g. 48 months at $3).
  - The text gives 2,400 Mcf gas at 78.8% methane. The box value uses 94% conversion.
  - The basis calls the 30 Mcf/d well "average". The Discussion gives the average stripper well as 15.6 Mcf/d.

## epa_2011_pro-902-increase-walking-survey-5-3-year-basis (2 pp.)
- **Extracted:**
  - Capex $1,000.
  - Opex-fixed $10,000/yr, as printed in the table.
  - A second capex row of $11,000 from the text.
  - The opex-fixed and $11,000 rows are both flagged SOURCE INCONSISTENT.
  - 1,500 Mcf/yr methane saved.
- **Price year:** 2011, assumed.
- **Source defects:**
  - The $10,000 incremental operating cost is printed as annual, but the paybacks (13 / 18 / 30 months) equal $11,000 / annual value, so it is treated as one-off. Taken as annual, the measure never pays back at $5 or $3.
  - The value of gas equals 1,500 × price, which values methane directly, despite the 94% whole-gas footnote.
  - The 1,500 Mcf/yr cannot be reproduced from the stated basis: 2,500 repairs × 0.5 scf/h × 8,760 h = 10,950 Mcf/yr.
  - The cost basis (a 2,900-mile partner system) differs from the savings basis (250,000 service connections).
  - DI&M is not ticked in the category box; "Other" is ticked instead.
