# Citation review

Generated by the build (`src/technology_database/pipeline/sources.py`). Every citation in the database was checked against the publisher, repository or agency page. This records what changed, what could not be confirmed, and which findings need a decision rather than an edit.

- **462 confirmed** — title, authors and publisher all matched a page that was actually fetched.
- **23 partial** — the document was identified but at least one field could not be seen verbatim.
- **13 unverified** — no reachable record. The citation is left as it was recorded, and says so.
- **112 carry a DOI.** A DOI is only ever what a fetched page showed. None was reconstructed from a pattern, so a blank means unconfirmed, not absent.

## Renames applied

Same document, different name. A citation names an organization as it was at publication, and an id keys on that citation, so a later rename of the issuer does not move the id. The retired id is superseded, not deleted, and its replacement is registered in the manifest with the reason.

| rename | why |
|---|---|
| `collodi-wheeler_2010_001 -> collodi_2010_001` | UKERC's 'Wheeler, F.' is the affiliation Foster Wheeler mis-parsed as a second author; Collodi is sole author |

## Where a web check lost to the document in hand

A reachable web page is evidence, not authority. Where a source is held locally the held copy decides, and the disagreement is recorded rather than quietly resolved.

- **etsap_2013_002** — the web check reported the IEA-ETSAP/IRENA heat pump brief as E12. The copy held in data/raw_data/technology_database/iea/etsap prints 'Technology Policy Brief E19 - January 2013' on page 1, and the modeller confirmed E19. The record follows the document; IRENA's website appears to post the same brief under E12.
