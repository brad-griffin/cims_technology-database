# EPA Natural Gas STAR Lessons Learned - extraction notes and source defects

Per-document notes from the 2026-10-02 extraction. Every cost and savings figure was checked against the rendered page. Page numbers are PDF pages.

## LL 2006 Replacing wet seals with dry seals (8 pp)
Extracted: two cases, (a) a fully functional wet seal and (b) an aging wet seal (Exh. 6A/6B), both on a 6-inch beam compressor. Recorded: capex 324,000; dry seal O&M 14,100; text O&M range; O&M savings of -88,300 (Exh. 4); seal cost per shaft-inch; the partner's 97 percent reduction; leak rates and ancillary costs in other-values.
Not extracted: the Exhibit 3 leakage chart, which is graphical.
SOURCE DEFECTS:
- Box paybacks of 29/18/13 months are gas-only (324,000 divided by the value of gas). Footnote b says the cost includes 102,400 of O&M reductions. Exhibit 7 gives 17/12/10 months, Exhibit 6A gives 10 months, and the executive summary says "as little as 11 months".
- The Exhibit 7 NPV at $7 is 1,288,009, but Exhibit 6A gives 1,228,009 for the same case. The 6A figure reproduces.
- Exhibit 5 prints "2 dry @ $13,000/shaft-inch" = 162,000. That rate gives 156,000; 162,000 corresponds to $13,500.
- The dry seal O&M of 14,100 used in Exhibits 5 and 6 is outside the text range of 8,400-14,000.
- Power is given in "kW per hour" (unit error).
- The Exhibit 6B subtitle says "with fully functional wet seals" for the aging-seal case (copy error).
- The Exhibit 5 row says "methane emissions reductions" but the figures are whole-gas volumes.

## LL 2006 Reducing methane emissions from compressor rod packing (8 pp)
Extracted: three cases (packing rings; rod plus rings; Consumers Energy). Recorded: box capex 540; 1,620; ring set range; 11,701 and 9,451 (both flagged); rod cost range; lifetimes (rings 3-5 yr, rod 10 yr); replacement thresholds in other-values.
SOURCE DEFECTS:
- Rod cost 9,451 + rings 1,620 = 11,071, but the text uses 11,701. The table paybacks fit 11,701.
- The text says 55 scfh = 1-year and 29 scfh = 2-year payback (and 376/137 scfh = 1-year/3-year), but Exhibits 3 and 4 print 7/12 months (and 7/18 months). The tables are simple paybacks. The doc's own DF formula gives about 32 scfh for a 1-year payback.
- The box "Implementation Cost" of $540 is the 1,620 ring cost annualised over 3 years, not a capital cost.

## LL 2006 Reducing emissions when taking compressors off-line (11 pp)

SOURCE DEFECTS:
- The box footnote and text call the Option 2/3 savings "incremental" over Option 1, but they are savings against the depressurized baseline.
- Exhibit 3a assigns rod packing 0.075 and blowdown valve 0.050 Mcf/h. The text gives rod packing 50 scfh and blowdown valve 75 scfh, so the components are swapped.
- The box footnote 1 says "10 percent discount rate", but the paybacks are simple.
- The Option 4 payback ignores the listed 1,575/yr operating cost.
- The Exhibit 5a/5c NPVs are the correct NPV divided by 1.1.
- The text calls P1V1 = P2V2 "Henry's Law" (it is Boyle's law).
